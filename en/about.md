At a time when health claims spread rapidly across social media, BiomedFacts was created to distinguish what is actually supported by evidence — and what is not.

## What you'll find here

The content covers topics including health, nutrition, news, laboratory tests, research and fact-checking of popular health claims.

The goal is not simply to reproduce scientific news, but to explain **what a study actually found, how strong the evidence is and what its limitations are**.

## How the content is supported

Articles are based on peer-reviewed scientific studies, systematic reviews and meta-analyses, official guidelines, and sources from recognised scientific and public health organisations.

Sources are provided so readers can examine the evidence and explore the subject further.

BiomedFacts is not intended to replace medical advice or provide personalised diagnoses or treatments. Its purpose is to inform and improve understanding of the science behind different health claims.
