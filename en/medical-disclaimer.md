The information published on this site is based on scientific literature, official guidelines and other reliable sources where possible. However, the content does not constitute and should not be considered personalised medical advice.

## Not a substitute for medical evaluation

BiomedFacts content does not replace diagnosis, assessment, treatment or advice from a doctor or another appropriately qualified healthcare professional.

The information provided should not be used for self-diagnosis, to start or stop treatment, or to change medication without first consulting a healthcare professional.

## Personalised health decisions

Appropriate medical care may vary significantly from one person to another depending on factors such as medical history, age, medication use, laboratory results and other individual circumstances.

For this reason, general information published by BiomedFacts cannot be used as a personalised recommendation.

## Medical emergencies

BiomedFacts is not an emergency medical service.

In the event of a medical emergency or serious symptoms, readers should seek immediate assistance from the appropriate emergency services or a healthcare professional.

## Evolving scientific knowledge

Medical and scientific knowledge evolves continuously. New evidence may change or add to previous conclusions.

Although efforts are made to keep content accurate and up to date, it cannot be guaranteed that every piece of information remains fully current at all times.

## External links

Articles may include links to scientific publications, organisations or other external websites.

A reference or link to an external source does not necessarily mean that BiomedFacts endorses all content, opinions or services provided by that website.

## Use of information

Use of the information provided through BiomedFacts is the responsibility of the reader.

For personal health concerns, diagnosis, treatment or changes to medication, advice should be sought from an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.
